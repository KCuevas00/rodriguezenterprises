About Us

One company. Four crafts.

Rodriguez Enterprises started from a simple idea: the people you trust with your biggest night, your property, and your home shouldn't have to be strangers to each other.

Our Story

Built one service at a time.

Rodriguez Enterprises grew out of Poured Up Vibez, a private bartending business built on the idea that great service is about more than what's in the glass. As the reputation for reliability grew, so did the ask — for security at those same events, for someone to fix a broken appliance, for a maintenance partner who actually shows up.

Rather than staying in one lane, Rodriguez Enterprises became the answer to all of it: one company, held to one standard, across four very different crafts.

What Stays the Same

Licensed. Local. Accountable.

Every service under Rodriguez Enterprises is run by trained, licensed professionals. Whether it's a bartender pouring your signature drink or a tech fixing your fridge, the standard doesn't change — show up, do it right, leave things better than you found them.
